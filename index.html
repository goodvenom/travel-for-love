Idukki district in Kerala

Kolukkumalai Peak

Best visited November to May; misty and low-visibility during the August–October monsoon. Go early for the clearest views — we woke up at 3 o'clock. A jeep ride (about an hour each way) gets you there; plan 3–4 hours total, or stay overnight to catch sunrise and sunset over the tea gardens, as we did.

Guna Caves, Kodaikanal

Guna Caves, Kodaikanal

Originally called Devil's Kitchen, Guna Caves became a popular sightseeing spot after featuring in Kamal Haasan's 1991 film Guna. The caves are fenced off for safety and only viewable from a distance. Timing: 8 AM–4 PM | Entry: ₹10 (₹20 for camera).

Vattakanal, Kodaikanal

Vattakanal Dolphin Nose Point

Dolphin Nose Point is 8 km off from Kodaikanal Lake. On the way you can enjoy Liril Falls and Vattakanal Waterfalls. No entrance or parking fees. Reaching this spot requires a moderate 3 km trek through the pines and rocks of the Palani Hill Range.

Dhanushkodi, Tamil Nadu coast

Dhanushkodi

Dhanushkodi is a unique Tamil Nadu coastal town known for its striking geography and haunting history. Left largely uninhabited after a 1964 cyclone, modern infrastructure upgrades have transformed it into an iconic destination. A July 9, 2023 road trip with friends captured unforgettable images of historic ruins and local shops.

Travel tips from the road

Useful notes from Kolukkumalai and Kodaikanal rides.

  • 1
    Kolukkumalai: start at 3 AMBest Nov–May. Jeep ~1 hr each way, plan 3–4 hrs, or stay overnight for sunrise & sunset.
  • 2
    Guna Caves: 8 AM–4 PMEntry ₹10 (₹20 camera). Fenced off for safety — view from a distance.
  • 3
    Dolphin Nose: allow for the trekAbout 3 km through pines and rocks. No entry or parking fees; Liril Falls and Vattakanal Waterfalls are on the way.
  • 4
    Kodaikanal Lake: 8 km awayDolphin Nose Point is around 8 km from the lake — plan the lake stop before or after the trek.
